# Merry Little Wishes

I made this app to give my girlfriend a simple place to save her Christmas
wishes—and to make finding the right gifts a little easier. Instead of keeping
track of scattered messages, screenshots, and links, she can put everything in
one list, including photos, prices, sizes, colors, and any other details that
matter.

The idea grew into something my mom and other family members could use too.
Each person has their own account and wishlist, with a link they can share
with gift-givers. That link stays up to date as their wishes change, and
gift-givers can mark things as purchased without spoiling the surprise.

It’s a personal project built around thoughtful gifts, fewer guesses, and a
little Christmas fun.
